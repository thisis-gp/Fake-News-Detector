Fake News Detector

A classroom text classifier: TF-IDF + logistic regression, trained on the Kaggle Fake and Real News dataset.

Read this first. This is a classroom project, not a fact checker. It scored about 98% on its own dataset, but that number is inflated by style cues: nearly all True articles in the data are from Reuters, so the model partly learns Reuters-style writing. It can be wrong, especially on other sources, topics or years. Do not use it to decide what is true.
