How it works

No chatbot and no black box. Four plain steps you can read in the source.

01

Clean

Lowercase the text, drop punctuation and common stop words.

02

Weigh words

TF-IDF scores each word by how distinctive it is across 38,639 articles.

03

Classify

Logistic regression turns those scores into a probability.

04

Explain

You see the words that pushed the result toward fake or real.

    The real numbers

    Measured on a held-out 20% of 38,639 deduplicated articles (Kaggle Fake and Real News).

    98.5%accuracy
    99.1%precision (fake)
    97.5%recall (fake)
    97.9%accuracy without Reuters cues
    Read this before you trust the score. The real articles in this dataset are almost all from one agency (Reuters), so a model can score high from style alone. That is a quirk of the data, not proof it can spot fake news in the wild. Expect it to be much weaker on other sources. Do not use it to decide what is true.
